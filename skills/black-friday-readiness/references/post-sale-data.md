# Collect now for the post-sale review

Record actual campaign dates and timezone, promoted SKUs, effective prices, stock snapshots and cost assumptions. Keep this preparation report so you can compare the plan with the outcome.

In your own secure storage, retain order line items, discounts, returns and refunds; advertising spend exports; Analytics reporting periods; and equivalent baseline periods. Do not add customer names, emails or addresses to this repository or an AI conversation. A later review can use locally anonymised identifiers if needed.

Review the sale after Cyber Monday, label the initial result provisional, and update after delayed conversions and returns. Reconcile advertising-platform conversion claims against store orders instead of adding platform totals together. This readiness tool does not collect orders or perform that later review.
